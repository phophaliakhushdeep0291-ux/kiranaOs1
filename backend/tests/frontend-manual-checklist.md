# KiranaOS Frontend Manual Checklist

Run this checklist after connecting the real frontend UI to the backend modules.

1. Add product
2. Delete product
3. Restore product from recycle bin
4. Remove cart item
5. Restore cart item
6. Clear cart
7. Restore cleared cart
8. Buyer Paid starts equal to bill total
9. Buyer Paid arrows increase/decrease properly
10. Buyer Paid never exceeds bill total
11. Changing quantity updates Buyer Paid to new total
12. Waived amount appears correctly
13. Confirm bill stores waived amount
14. Reports include waived amount
15. Offline bill goes pending sync
16. Online reconnect syncs pending bill
