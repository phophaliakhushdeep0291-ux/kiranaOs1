# WhatsApp copy

Short enough to forward, specific enough to be believed. Replace every
`{{placeholder}}` before sending. See [README.md](README.md) for the claims
policy.

> **Send these to people who know you, or who asked.** Bulk-messaging strangers
> gets the number banned within days and is a poor first impression besides.

## 6. Answers to the four questions you will always get

**"Net nahi chalta hamare yahan."**

```
Bilkul isi liye banaya hai. Billing poori tarah aapke phone/
computer par chalti hai — net ki zaroorat sirf backup ke liye
hai. Net band ho to bill bante rahenge, aur jab bhi net aayega
sab apne aap upload ho jayega. Ek bhi bill nahi jayega.
```

**"Mera data safe hai?"**

```
Data aapka hai. Do jagah rehta hai — aapke device par, aur
aapke apne cloud backup mein. Doosri dukaan aapka data kabhi
nahi dekh sakti. Chhodna chahein to apna pura data export kar
ke le ja sakte hain.
```

**"Purana hisaab kaise aayega?"**

```
Customers aur products ek saath import ho jate hain, aur har
customer ka purana udhar balance opening balance ki tarah daal
sakte hain. Shuru karne mein aadha ghanta lagta hai — main
saath baith ke kara doonga.
```

**"Mujhe computer nahi aata."**

```
Phone chalana aata hai to yeh bhi aa jayega. Bill banane mein
teen tap lagte hain. Pura app Hindi mein hai. Aur agar kahin
atak jayein to app ke andar hi "Ask Artha" hai — sawaal likhiye,
jawaab aapki hi dukaan ke hisaab se milega.
```
