| Placeholder | What it is |
|---|---|

| `{{WEBSITE}}` | Your signup or download link |

## What this copy claims — and why it stops there

Every feature named below is one that exists in the product today. I wrote the
copy from the codebase, not from a wishlist.

**Claimed, because it is built:** offline billing that keeps working with no
internet and syncs when the connection returns; GST invoices and a GST summary
report; udhar (credit) khata with per-customer outstanding and payment
recording; barcode scan-to-cart; thermal/80mm and normal printer support;
multi-device and multi-counter use with cloud backup; daily closing with cash
drawer reconciliation; stock, purchase bills and suppliers; expenses; offers,
loyalty and gift cards; staff logins with permissions; customer QR self-order;
reports exportable to CSV, PDF and Excel; a built-in help assistant that answers
from your own shop's data; English and Hindi throughout; and 11 shop types —
kirana, clothing, footwear, auto parts & hardware, electronics & mobiles,
pharmacy, stationery, furniture, beauty & cosmetics, restaurant & café, and a
custom option.

**Deliberately NOT claimed anywhere in these files:**

- **No customer counts, shop counts, or "trusted by X shops".** Use a real
  number once you have one, and only then.
- **No uptime, accuracy or speed percentages.** "99.9% uptime" is a contractual
  promise, not a slogan.
- **No testimonials or star ratings.** Invented ones are unlawful under India's
  Consumer Protection Act (unfair trade practice) and destroy trust when found.
- **No awards, certifications, press mentions, or "government approved".**
- **No "GST compliant" as an absolute.** The app produces GST invoices and a GST
  summary; it is **not** a statutory audit or filing product, and it does not
  file returns for anyone. Saying "GST bill in one tap" is true. Saying
  "guaranteed GST compliance" is not, and invites a complaint you cannot answer.
- **No competitor names** — no "better than X". Comparative advertising in India
  must be substantiated, and naming a rival invites a disparagement notice.
- **No income promises** ("double your profit", "save ₹50,000 a year"). If you
  want a savings claim, base it on a real, documented customer and say whose it
  is.

If you add a claim later, the test is simple: **could you prove it to an annoyed
customer six months from now?** If not, cut it.

## Two practical notes

**The trust line matters more than the feature list.** Shopkeepers have been
burned by software that lost their data or stopped working when the internet
did. The single strongest thing this product has is that billing genuinely does
not stop when the network does. Lead with that everywhere.

**On the name.** "Artha" is a common word in Indian business and finance naming,
and it is crowded in fintech. Before you spend money on printing, signage or
ads, it is worth a trademark search and a domain/handle check — a rename after
10,000 pamphlets is an expensive lesson.
