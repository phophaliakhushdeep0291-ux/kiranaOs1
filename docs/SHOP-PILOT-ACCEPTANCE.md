# First shop pilot: record actual outcomes

Start with one independent shop and one counter. Agree a quiet supervised trial window with its owner. Keep the current billing process available until the owner reconciles the trial. Use a dedicated demo account for practice; issue each real sale only once through the agreed live system.

Record the date, shop type, app build, API revision, devices, printer model and participating owner/accountant. A result is pending until someone records the observed outcome and evidence.

| Check | Acceptance | Result / evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening setup | Owner verifies 20 representative products, units/packs, prices, stock and 5 customer/supplier balances against source records. | Pending |
| Normal counter | Scan/search, bill, cash/change and recorded external payment agree with independently calculated totals. | Pending |
| Interrupted connection | Save two distinct sales and a credit collection offline. Pending status is visible. Reconnect and retry: exactly one server copy of each, matching stock and debt on both counters. | Pending |
| Rejected sync | Conflicting or disallowed changes stay visibly unresolved, with no silent loss of the local evidence. Owner resolves them before closing. | Pending |
| Returns and damage | Resellable return restores stock; damaged goods do not become saleable stock. Refund, stock loss and posted journal amounts match original records. | Pending |
| Assistant | Owner uses ten real phrases, including unclear products/units and the intended languages. Assistant clarifies missing details, previews changes, respects PIN/role requirements and reports actual outcomes. Record misunderstandings. | Pending |
| Printer and Tally | Physically print and rescan a receipt/barcode. Import a synthetic day into a separate Tally test company and reconcile vouchers, tax, debt and stock; retry must not duplicate entries. | Pending |
| Closing | Count cash and compare cash/external payments, customer debt, supplier balances, sales/returns, physical stock and accounting sources. Every difference is explained; ledger integrity alone is insufficient. | Pending |
| Recovery | Restore an encrypted copy into a separate disposable environment; compare records, money, stock and audit history. Measure recovery time. Verify access to the separately stored encryption key. | Pending |
| Owner decision | Owner can bill and find an error without developer guidance, and agrees how support, daily backups and incident escalation will work. | Pending |

Stop live use for unexplained money/stock differences, lost or duplicated transactions, inaccessible pending sales, or unauthorized actions. Preserve evidence and use the agreed fallback while fixing the issue.

Current acceptance boundaries: live voice needs a configured provider and physical microphone tests; recorded external payments do not charge a card; Tally and printers need their actual installations; UAE remains a preview. Opening balances, historical corrections, complete inventory valuation and statutory filings need accountant review. A local restore drill does not establish offsite recovery or production recovery-time guarantees.
